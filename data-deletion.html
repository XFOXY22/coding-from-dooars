Data Deletion Instructions

Instructions for requesting deletion of personal information associated with a Coding From Dooars account.

Last updated: September 18, 2026

You can request deletion of personal information associated with your Coding From Dooars account by contacting us using the process below.

1. How to Request Data Deletion

Send an email to codingfromdooars@gmail.com with the subject Data Deletion Request.

Please include the email address associated with your Coding From Dooars account and enough information for us to identify the account and understand your request.

2. What Happens After a Request

We will review the request and verify that it relates to the account or information identified in the request. Where deletion is applicable, personal information will be deleted or de-identified as required by the applicable process and legal or operational obligations.

3. Information That May Need to Be Retained

Certain information may need to be retained where required for legitimate operational, security, legal, accounting, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, or other applicable obligations. Such retained information will not be kept longer than reasonably necessary for the applicable purpose.

4. Project Files and Orders

If your account contains project files, order records, or delivery information, the deletion request may affect information associated with those services. We may need to retain limited records where required for legitimate operational or legal purposes.

5. Facebook Login Users

If you used Facebook Login to access Coding From Dooars, mention that in your deletion request and provide the account email or other identifying information available to you. This helps us locate the relevant Coding From Dooars account.

6. Contact

For questions about a deletion request, contact codingfromdooars@gmail.com.
