Privacy Policy

How Coding From Dooars handles account, order, project, and service-related information.

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Coding From Dooars respects the privacy and responsible handling of information associated with customers, accounts, orders, projects, and services provided through the website.

1. Information We May Handle

Depending on how you use the website, information may include account details, contact information, order information, project requirements, files or content submitted for a project, and information needed to provide customer support and project delivery.

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used for authentication, marketplace functionality, order processing, project delivery, account management, customer support, security, and operation of the website.

3. Authentication and Account Information

When you create or use an account, information associated with that account may be processed to authenticate you, maintain your account, identify your orders, and provide account-related features.

4. Orders and Project Information

Information connected with an order may be used to understand submitted requirements, process the order, manage project progress, provide requested customization, deliver the project, and provide support.

5. Customer-Submitted Files

If a project requires customer files or media, those materials may be processed and stored as necessary to provide the requested service. Customers should only submit content they are permitted to provide and use.

6. Service Providers and Technology

Coding From Dooars uses technology and service infrastructure for website authentication, database functionality, project-related file storage, and delivery. These services may process information only as necessary for their applicable functions and service operation.

7. Access Control

Administrative and customer-related information is not intended to be openly accessible to every website visitor. Access is controlled according to the platform's role-based permissions and operational requirements.

8. Responsible Use of Information

Information available through the website should only be accessed, processed, copied, disclosed, modified, or used for legitimate Coding From Dooars website and service purposes.

9. Security and Limitations

Reasonable technical and organizational measures may be used to protect website and customer information. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security against every possible risk.

10. Data Deletion

You may request deletion of personal information associated with your account. Please follow the instructions on our Data Deletion page.

11. Privacy Questions

For privacy-related questions or concerns, contact codingfromdooars@gmail.com. Please include enough information for the request to be understood and handled appropriately.

12. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the website, services, technology, or applicable requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.
